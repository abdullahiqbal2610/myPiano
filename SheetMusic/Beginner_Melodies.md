# Virtual Piano Melodies 🎵

Here is your personal sheet music! Since you are using the laptop keyboard, I have translated famous beginner-friendly piano melodies directly into the exact characters you need to press. 

*Hint: Play the letters from left to right. Commas (`,`) mean a short pause, and spaces mean a normal pause.*

### 1. Twinkle Twinkle Little Star
The absolute classic to get your fingers used to the keys.
> **A A G G H H G** 
> **F F D D S S A**

### 2. Ode to Joy (Beethoven)
A beautiful, uplifting melody that strictly uses the white keys.
> **D D F G , G F D S**
> **A A S D , D S S**
>
> **D D F G , G F D S**
> **A A S D , S A A**

### 3. Jingle Bells
Fast and fun!
> **D D D , D D D , D G A S D**
> **F F F , F F D D , D S S D S , G**

### 4. Happy Birthday
Great for practicing jumping back and forth!
> **A A S A F D**
> **A A S A G F**
> **A A K H F D S**

### 5. Mary Had a Little Lamb
Very simple, mostly using just three keys.
> **D S A S D D D**
> **S S S , D G G**
> **D S A S D D D**
> **S S D S A**

---

### Reference: Your Keyboard Layout
For your reference, here is exactly how your laptop keys map to the real piano notes:
- **A** = C (Do)
- **S** = D (Re)
- **D** = E (Mi)
- **F** = F (Fa)
- **G** = G (Sol)
- **H** = A (La)
- **J** = B (Ti)
- **K** = C (High Do)

*The black keys are in the row above (W, E, T, Y, U).*
